# What's new

Short notes for the next build, shown in the app after updating. Edit these before each build.

- Surprise Me: a new treasure chest with a lantern inside; your pick rises out of it on a parchment card with rating, genres and a short description
- View details and Try again are now red and blue brass plates
- Search keeps Movies and TV Shows separate, and opens on the one you used last
- New corner pennant on Search, and the EARNED ribbon with laurels on bounties
- Captain's Quarters now has wooden stairs and new lanterns
- Read: carved header shelf, a parchment Continue Reading panel and the category name on a scroll

- Bounties are cartoon wanted posters on parchment, two across on phones, with laurels beside EARNED

- The Search sail is now a small corner accent
- Read: unread chapter counts are small parchment tags, and rope rigging decorates the header
- New Appearance & Motion settings: animations, haptic feedback, sound effects (off by default), fewer decorations and a low-power mode
- The compass replaces loading spinners, with "Loading your voyage…" on Home and Read
- Themed empty screens in Watch, Ani-Drama and Read ("Your library awaits" with an Add a book button)
- Offline Voyage: the ship progress bar, sizes as it downloads, round pause and resume buttons, a red Retry, and a Wi-Fi only switch
- Downloads wait for Wi-Fi when "Download over Wi-Fi only" is on
- Folding, unfolding or rotating no longer reloads Read, so lists keep their place
- Lighter artwork files for less memory use
