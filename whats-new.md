# What's new

Short notes for the next build, shown in the app after updating. Edit these before each build.

- The app is now MugiVerse: new name, the straw-hat MugiVerse logo with a WATCH / ANI-DRAMA ribbon, and the Three Tides opening animation
- New bottom bar with a compass in the middle: Home · Assistant · 🧭 · My List · Settings on Watch and Ani-Drama, Library · Updates · 🧭 · History · Browse · More on Read
- Tap the compass to switch: the ship (Watch), the fruit (Ani-Drama) or the chest (Read). Back, the compass or a tap outside closes it
- App updates now show on the 🔔 at the top. Tap the logo to choose what Home shows, as before
- Settings → MugiVerse look turns it off and brings back the Fenrir look
