# What's new

Short notes for the next build, shown in the app after updating. Edit these before each build.

- Fixed: shelves could show up without their wood on some categories
- A whole new library look: a carved wooden crown with the wolf medallion at the top, with FENRIR READ ▾ and Read ▾ set in gold letters into the wood
- Continue reading sits in an ornate gold cabinet, with your book standing behind a tinted glass door
- Every book is now an ornate tome: your real cover in a metal frame, with runes, gems and a ribbon in that cover's colours
- A glowing wolf tome after your books takes you to Browse to add a book
- New decorations on the shelves (wolf bookends, moon lamp, crystals, lanterns, katanas, a dragon and more), spotlights under each shelf and ivy at the top
- The island at the bottom of the Read side now has the same see-through glass as Watch
- Tapping a book turns the tome round to its back (wolf and FENRIR in the book's colour), then its page opens
- The Continue reading book leaves the cabinet as a tome, then opens to your chapter
- The crown (and the Watch / Read switch) is only on the Library now
- Updates, History, Browse, More, book pages and Settings use the library's colours: warm dark brown, gold titles and headings, gold-edged buttons and filters
- The unread count is a small tag on the corner of each book instead of on the cover art
- Book titles are centred under their books
- History: the last covers can scroll up above the island
- The ivy is smaller and stays in the corners, off your books
- The ★ rating moved next to "% read", so it no longer covers the cover art
