# What's new

Short notes for the next build, shown in the app after updating. Edit these before each build.

- New: Private reading — keep books out of Continue, Surprise Me and Captain's Log on the Watch side (Settings → Privacy); unlock with your fingerprint or PIN
- New: Continue has All / Watch / Ani-Drama / Read tabs and opens on the mode you came from
- New: Private Library (the 🔒 in Read's header) — move books into a locked cabinet; they disappear from the library, history, updates and notifications until you unlock
- New: Read privacy settings — lock all of Read, lock when leaving the app, hide private notifications, incognito
- New: collector's-edition books on the shelves, with a bookmark showing unread / reading / finished (can be switched back)
- New: Continue — everything unfinished from Watch, Ani-Drama and Read in one place (See all › on Continue Watching / Continue Reading)
- New: Captain's Log — streaks, reading and watching time, weekly chart and top genre (Settings, or More on Read)
- New: Bounties — wanted-poster milestones you earn as you read and watch (inside Captain's Log)
- New: Offline Voyage — get the next chapters ready for a trip and see every download in one place (My List download button, or More on Read)
- New: Surprise Me — open the treasure chest for something to watch or read (chest next to the bell, and on Discover)
- Fixed: Search posters now have their thin gold frame (violet in Ani-Drama)
- MugiVerse History: reading-log look with this week's stats, dates and gold-framed covers (list or grid)
- MugiVerse Updates: parchment dates, NEW badges and gold download buttons
- MugiVerse Search: red search bar, Discover banner, Movies / TV Shows switch and gold-framed posters (violet in Ani-Drama)
- MugiVerse Read: More moved to the top (next to the bell); bigger Continue Reading text and shelf name
- MugiVerse My List: new nautical look with Saved / Cloud, All / Movies / Series / Anime, seven sort options, search in the header and gold-framed posters
- MugiVerse Browse: gold tabs, Novel / Manga pills and two-column source cards with a Latest button
- Fixed: big empty gaps between some paragraphs in Paged reading
- Ani-Drama: new poster cards (rounder violet frame, bold score and DUB badges, bold titles) and the final colour palette
- Ani-Drama: tap the MugiVerse logo (ANI-DRAMA ▾) for the same menu as on Watch
- MugiVerse: Movies / TV Shows toggle now sits in the header
- Search moved to the bottom bar; Assistant is in the compass menu
- Ani-Drama: "Anime ▾" picker in the header, filters in one swipeable row, refreshed calendar
- New: Line spacing and Paragraph spacing controls (− / +) in the reader's How to read sheet
- Fixed: in Paged reading, the chapter title is always at the top of the first page, even when a site puts an author's note before it
- Fixed: the gold "Tap the logo" tip no longer covers the opening animation or the What's new card

- New opening: three trails (red, purple, gold) sweep in, meet in a gold compass flash, and the MugiVerse logo opens out from the middle (about 2.5 s; shorter when your phone's animations are off)
- The launch screen now shows the straw-hat compass on dark navy instead of the old Fenrir wolf, on Watch and Read
- Novel page mode: the page slider jumps by whole pages, and a tablet or unfolded phone held sideways shows two pages side by side like an open book (they turn two at a time)
- Read: a carved beam with a soft shadow along the bottom of the top bar, so the shelves slide under it smoothly when you scroll
- Compass menu: light pulses run along each coloured line from the compass up into the ship, fruit and chest while the menu is open
